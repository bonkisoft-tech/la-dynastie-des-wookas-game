The Project

Enter the world of Wookas.

La Dynastie des Wookas — The Game is a game adaptation project developed by Bonkisoft Games in collaboration with Laurie-Alice Dumas, author of the original literary work.


The project explores how the world, characters, atmosphere and imagination of Wookas can evolve into an interactive fantasy adventure.

A world built for exploration.

The game is being developed as an evolving project, beginning with exploration, story, interaction, objects, puzzles and world discovery.


Early development focuses on discovering how Wookas translates into an interactive experience.

Visual Direction

A glimpse into the Wookas world.

The current visual direction combines lush natural environments, magical architecture, glowing portals, waterfalls and a cinematic fantasy atmosphere.
