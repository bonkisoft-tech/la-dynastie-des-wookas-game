The Project

Enter the world of Wookas.

La Dynastie des Wookas — The Game is a game adaptation project developed by Bonkisoft Games in collaboration with Laurie-Alice Dumas, author of the original literary work.


The project explores how the world, characters, atmosphere and imagination of Wookas can evolve into an interactive fantasy adventure.

A world built for exploration.

Early development focuses on exploration, story, interaction, objects, puzzles and discovery.


The project is being developed as an evolving game concept, beginning with a small playable experience and expanding over time.
